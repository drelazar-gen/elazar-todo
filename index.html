@todo functions — quick guide

Type @todo anywhere in a note and a menu of every function pops up — keep typing to filter it, use ↑/↓ + Enter or just click. Tags marked Instant apply the moment you hit Enter. Tags marked Hourly need a quick read from the assistant and apply within about an hour.

@todo make recurringInstant

Type it and a picker pops up right there — Daily, Weekly, Monthly, or Custom (every N days). Once set, the item resets back to unchecked on that schedule automatically, whether or not you'd checked it off.

@todo delegate to [Name]Instant

Opens a picker: search your contacts (or add a new one on the spot), then choose reminder nudges — Manual (you send them yourself from the Delegated tab) or Auto with an interval (Daily/Weekly/Monthly/Custom). Sends them their own private checklist link by email and shows up in your Delegated tab so you can see whether they've checked it off.

@todo archive when doneInstant

The moment you check this item off, it moves itself to the Archived tab instead of sitting checked-off in your main list. Unarchive it any time from that tab.

remind me in 3 days / next Tuesday / etc.Hourly

Brings the item back to your attention (re-flagged, noted as recalled) at that time — whether or not it's already checked off.

@todo escalate if still open ...Hourly

Only fires if the item is still unchecked at that time — good for "make sure this actually got done" follow-ups.

EVENT: ...Hourly

Start an item's title with EVENT: and it gets pushed onto your Google Calendar automatically.

@context (or any other @word)Hourly

General-purpose flag for "this note needs the assistant to actually do something with it," reviewed on the same hourly pass.
